Karakpekarcha Privacy Policy

Developer: FangedFelix
Privacy and support: homernavserver@gmail.com

What the app does
Karakpekarcha helps you track expenses, budgets, accounts, recurring payments, manual investments and personal debts. It does not provide banking, lending, trading or payment execution. This policy describes the Android app. You can use manual entry and supported file imports without connecting Gmail or enabling the assistant.

Data on your device
Transactions, amounts, currencies, merchants, account details, categories, notes, receipts, budgets, debts, portfolio records, rules and review history are stored locally. The app can retain financial SMS text and sanitised email excerpts as evidence for a transaction or review item. The on-device categoriser learns from your choices and stores model counts locally; it does not send training records to a server.

SMS and bank notifications
If you grant SMS access, Karakpekarcha reads historical and incoming SMS to detect financial transactions, including while the app is closed or not in use. Sender and content filters process financial alerts locally. SMS access does not allow this app to send SMS. You can deny or revoke SMS permissions in Android Settings and continue with manual entry and imports. Optional notification access reads supported bank-app alerts when enabled in Android Settings; turn it off there to stop access.

Contacts
Contact lookup is optional and used on the device to replace payment identifiers with contact names. Contact records are not uploaded by this feature. Denying or revoking permission leaves the original payment identifiers visible.

Optional Gmail connection
Google sign-in and Gmail read-only authorization are handled through Google services. When you connect a mailbox, the app requests email/profile information needed to identify the connected mailbox and uses the Gmail API to fetch messages matching your enabled bank senders. Gmail access is broader than the app's sender filter; the app restricts its own queries and validates senders. It does not send or delete email. Google processes authorization and API requests under its own policies. Disconnect in Settings to stop syncing and remove the app's local connection state; you can also revoke access in your Google Account. Imported transactions remain until you delete them. Test authorizations may need reconnection after seven days.

Optional assistant
The assistant uses your API key for your selected Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic service. Saving a key alone does not enable requests. After disclosure and opt-in, you choose eligible material for review, see the prepared payload and explicitly send it. A request may contain sanitised financial message text, transaction amounts and currencies, merchant/account hints, and category names. Sanitisation reduces identifying content; it does not guarantee anonymity. Cancelled previews are not sent. Automatic background category requests are disabled. The selected provider receives the approved request and network metadata such as your IP address, processes it to return suggestions, and applies its own retention and security terms. The app does not use these requests for advertising. Removing the key or disabling the assistant prevents future requests, but does not erase requests already received by a provider.

Other network access
Exchange-rate lookup contacts open.er-api.com to obtain conversion rates and sends currency lookup information and ordinary network metadata, not your transaction ledger. Google authentication components communicate with Google when used. There is no project-operated synchronization backend or advertising/analytics SDK configured in this release source. Device-only Groups do not send invitations or synchronize balances.

Exports, backups and support
Manual exports and optional scheduled backups write sensitive files to a location you select. A cloud document provider may upload those files under its own settings. Android automatic cloud/device-transfer backup of app data is disabled. In-app backup files exclude credentials and derived categoriser counts. You control sharing of exported files and diagnostic reports. The support report previews build information and aggregate counts; it does not upload automatically. Alert sample exports can still contain sensitive context, so review them before sharing. If you email support, the email and any attachments are received by the developer through Gmail and retained as needed to resolve the request; request deletion using the address above.

Retention and deletion
Local records remain until removed or the app's storage is cleared. Some transaction deletions are reversible and keep a hidden record. For complete local removal, use Android Settings > Apps > Karakpekarcha > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall. Disconnect Gmail and revoke its Google Account grant separately if you want that authorization removed as well. Remove assistant credentials in the app; provider-side request retention follows the provider's terms. Clearing the app does not delete files you previously exported, copies held by your chosen storage provider, support emails or data already sent to a provider. Delete those copies separately or contact their recipient. Karakpekarcha does not create a developer-hosted user account; connecting Gmail is an optional third-party authorization.

Security
Android app isolation protects local app data; optional biometric/device-credential lock restricts app access. Assistant keys use Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. Network requests use HTTPS. The full transaction database and exported backups are not represented as end-to-end encrypted. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and exports protected.
